## Refund meaning

Enhancement prices are army points charged in Effective at Muster, not Supply purchases. Removing an assignment frees its points for subsequent compositions and removes its Stage binding. It never credits Supply, RC, Recovery or an earlier transfer fee. The impact deduplicates the same bearer appearing in both starter and Stage assignments and counts each Upgrade bearer separately. Campaign equipment, Honours, Scars and completed battle snapshots are preserved.

For a battle-only detachment toggle, incompatible choices are removed atomically from that local Muster draft and its points recalculate. Stage bindings persist until the army Package changes or a legal transfer occurs. Previously a second draft update accidentally restored the removed Enhancement.

## Verification and limits

19 focused regression tests exercise upgrades and aftermath through the shared command engine; the prior Ossuary Key test was corrected to the canonical one-use rule. The two-account browser integration additionally verifies the warning before saving, removal/refund through Auth → Edge → Postgres, an unchanged Supply wallet and no automatic restoration on returning the detachment.

Desktop navigation is compact and was checked at 1280×640 without inner scrolling; the mobile navigation remains accessible at 390×844 without horizontal overflow. Very short desktop windows retain scrolling so no tabs are clipped.

The app validates and records campaign consequences, costs, choices and uses. Warhammer attacks, movement, target/range and applicable core roll restrictions are still resolved at the physical table using the rule text in the battle packet. Tracking a use does not execute a save, attack or restoration roll.

Previously completed battles are historical records and are not recalculated by deployment. A separately agreed result correction uses the saved-dice revision flow.
